## Why this exists
The site's credibility — and therefore its entire value, including any future revenue — comes from
**not selling**. The moment an association makes the analysis look bought, the trust that makes the site
worth reading is gone. This policy lets LCS point readers toward genuinely good tools (the "bridge"
idea) *without* becoming a marketing arm. The test behind every rule below: **"Would this still be here
if we had nothing to sell?"** If a page only makes sense as a conversion mechanism, it fails.

## Scope
Applies any time LCS links, refers, recommends, is sponsored by, earns referral/affiliate revenue from,
or white-labels content with an external product or company — the bridges (e.g., Cadena, River,
Unchained), sponsorships, affiliate links, and content partnerships.

## Who we'll associate with — partner qualification (ALL must hold)
1. **Bitcoin-only.** No altcoin or multi-crypto firms. A partner that also pushes altcoins is
   disqualifying regardless of terms.
2. **Best-of-breed, high-integrity.** The clear quality/integrity leader in its category — the one
   you'd point a friend to.
3. **Genuinely good for the reader — you'd feature it with ZERO referral revenue.** The single hardest
   test and the most important one.
4. **Clean track record.** No history of harming users (rehypothecation blow-ups, custody failures,
   misleading marketing). Self-custody-friendly and transparent where relevant.
5. **Values-aligned.** Supports user sovereignty and doesn't require you to soften the truth.

## How we associate — the "bridge, not billboard" standard
- **Educate, don't send.** A bridge *models and explains how the product works — including its failure
  states and risks* — never a bare "sign up here." The page must teach something true and useful even to
  a reader who never clicks through.
- **Tell the whole truth.** State the partner's shortcomings and risks as plainly as for any other topic
  (POSITIONING §1.5). If you find you can't say anything critical about a partner, you're too close —
  stop.
- **Editorial control is absolute.** No partner reviews, edits, approves, or pre-clears content. The
  analysis and any verdict are never for sale.
- **No gag exclusivity.** Never accept terms that prevent covering competitors or telling the truth
  about the partner or its category.

## Disclosure
- **Disclose every paid/referral relationship clearly and visibly on the page** — in plain sight, not a
  buried footnote. Disclosure is a trust move, not a compliance chore.
- **Distinguish** paid/referral relationships from unpaid recommendations, so a reader always knows which
  is which.

## Hard exclusions (no matter the money)
- Altcoin / multi-crypto sponsors.
- Anything requiring you to soften or omit honest risk disclosure.
- Pay-for-positive-coverage, or any arrangement that buys the verdict.
- Exclusivity that gags competitor coverage.
- **Brand-association that makes you look like a paid spokesperson** (the Wave/Elevate likeness lesson) —
  being *used in a partner's marketing* is different from *pointing readers to a good product*; the
  former is out.
- The **affiliate-directory / comparison-farm** model — a page whose reason to exist is the referral
  links. Dilutes credibility; excluded.

## Before adding any partner — the checklist
1. Does it pass **all** qualification criteria above?
2. **Would I feature this with zero referral revenue?** (If no → stop.)
3. Have I written its **risks and shortcomings** honestly on the page?
4. Is the **disclosure** clear and visible?
5. Does the page **stand on its own as education** if the reader never clicks?
6. Does the core education stay **free and un-gated**?

If any answer is no, don't ship it.

## Ongoing
- **Review partners periodically.** A partner that degrades (changes terms, harms users, drifts toward
  altcoins) gets dropped — and the change noted honestly to readers.
- **The education is never "top-of-funnel."** A bridge points to a solution as a *service to the reader*;
  the site is not a funnel to a product, and no internal framing should treat it as one.

## Relationship to the funding strategy
This policy is what makes **Path 2 (the bridge model)** pursuable without compromising the neutrality
that is the site's moat. It is deliberately strict: the bridge model preserves *control*, but it is not
integrity-*free* — it trades the AM-neutrality risk for a referral-neutrality risk that this standard
exists to actively manage. Pair with `FUNDING_STRATEGY.md` and `REACH_GROWTH_PLAN.md` (bridges only pay
off at scale, so this standard has room to be tested on small, clearly-worthy partners first).
