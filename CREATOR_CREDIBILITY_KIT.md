## What this is for
Build these **once**, reuse everywhere. A single coherent story plus a few assets removes the friction
from every reach, funding, and recognition move you'll make.

## 1. The narrative (drafted — site voice)
**Elevator (one sentence):** Rigorous, no-hype Bitcoin investment education — free interactive tools that
show their work, so anyone can check the case for Bitcoin as an asset for themselves.

**The ~130-word case** (reuse verbatim for the Geyser page, a Satos nomination, and partner intros):
> Most Bitcoin content is either hype or dismissal — neither of which helps someone genuinely trying to
> decide whether it belongs in their portfolio. This platform takes the opposite approach: rigorous,
> no-hype analysis with free, interactive tools that show their work — every number reproducible, every
> assumption stated, the downside shown as plainly as the upside. The goal isn't to persuade but to let a
> serious person check the reasoning for themselves, whether they're new to Bitcoin or a skeptic looking
> to poke holes. The case for Bitcoin as an asset is already strong enough that it doesn't need
> embellishing — it needs honest, checkable presentation, which is scarce. Funding keeps these tools
> free, ad-free, and independent — accountable to the analysis, not to advertisers or referrals — so the
> work stays trustworthy as it reaches more people.

**What makes it different (3 bullets):**
- Every number is reproducible and every assumption stated — downside shown as plainly as upside.
- Built for the reader to *verify*, not to be persuaded — serves both newcomers and skeptics.
- No hype, no funnel, no ads — accountable to the analysis, not to advertisers or referrals.

including education and media; anyone can nominate anyone; no fee found; the Satos Council's stated
ethos: contributors who *"refuse to build cages, and instead build exits."*). The Las Vegas 2026
ceremony (Apr 29) has passed; the **Hong Kong 2026 cycle's nominations open ~July 27, 2026**. The site
(TheSatosAwards.com) is a JS app — the exact HK deadline and category names must be read in-browser.

**When the window opens (~Jul 27) — the 5-minute procedure:**
1. Visit **thesatosawards.com/nominate** in your browser.
2. Confirm the HK cycle's **category list** — target the **Education** category (fallback: Media /
   Content, whatever the exact names are). Note the **voting dates** while you're there.
3. Paste the texts below into the form. Nominee: **→ JM's call** — nominate **the site** (Last Coin
   Standing, lastcoinstanding.com) with you as founder, or yourself as educator; community awards
   usually honor either. The site is the safer first-cycle choice (the brand is the asset).
4. Anyone can nominate — you may self-nominate, but a **peer nomination reads stronger**: if one or two
   Bitcoiner mutuals on X would submit it, that's worth a DM. (This is the relationship layer earning
   its first keep.)

**One-liner (form short-description field):**

> reproducible, every assumption stated, the downside as plain as the upside.

**Nomination statement (~120 words, Satos-tuned — honest, sovereignty-framed, no embellishment):**
> Last Coin Standing (lastcoinstanding.com) is a free, independent Bitcoin education site built on one
> discipline: show the math, honestly. Its interactive tools — a retirement calculator, allocation and

> price history, with every assumption stated and every number reproducible in the reader's own
> spreadsheet. The downside is shown as plainly as the upside. There are no ads, no referrals, no
> token, nothing for sale. It exists so an ordinary person deciding whether Bitcoin belongs in their
