## 1. Charter — what X is for (and not for)

Three jobs, in order:
1. **Traffic with intent** — send people to the live tools, where the site does the persuading.
2. **The relationship layer** — genuine standing with the small set of people whose reshares, letters,
   votes, and intros gate everything in FUNDING_STRATEGY.
3. **The proof-of-work record** — a public trail of rigorous, both-sided output that any grant
   reviewer, award juror, or potential partner can scroll.

Not jobs: follower-count maximization, engagement farming, price calls, altcoin dunking, drama.
The account is the site in miniature — an instrument, not a pitch (the internal rationale; on X as on
the pages, we demonstrate it rather than say it).

## 2. Time budget & cadence (sustainable inside the ~40-hour week)

Site work stays primary; X gets a bounded, daily-touch allocation JM can actually sustain — and can
scale up when engagement makes it feel less like effort (JM's own prediction):

- **Daily, ~2 × 15–20 min** (morning + evening): replies and engagement in the target circles (§6),
  plus capture of anything reshare-worthy. This is the real growth engine at small follower counts —
  a good reply under a 50k-follower account out-reaches anything posted to our own timeline.
- **Weekly, one anchor thread** (~60–90 min including chart prep — mostly reuse of page assets).
  Steady drumbeat beats bursts; one excellent thread a week is a strong cadence for a rigor brand.
- **2–4 standalone posts/week** from the formats in §4 — each ≤15 min because the asset already
  exists (camera exports, live figures).
- **Weekly 15-min review** (§8). Nothing else is scheduled; everything else is opportunistic.

Nostr (Damus, already set up): mirror the anchor thread and the best standalone posts, ~5 min/week.
Zero-strategy mirroring for now; Nostr grows into its own lane only if signal appears.

## 3. Voice & guardrails

The site's register, compressed for the feed:
- **Plain-spoken, numbers first, both sides always.** Every strong claim ships with its mirror (the
  backtest discipline). The unusual credibility move on X is showing the negative case unprompted.
- **Every number verifiable** — ideally by clicking through and running the tool. Never post a figure
  the page can't reproduce.
- **No price predictions, no timing calls, no "buy now."** State what the arithmetic implies under a
  stated assumption; the assumption is always named. "The record, not a forecast" travels well.
- **Credit generously and by name** (house habit: Porkopolis/Mežinskis, Krueger, Foss, Walton,
  @DarkSide2030). On X this is also relationship-building — credit is the highest-signal mention.
- **No embellishment** (POSITIONING §1.5): the case is already overwhelming. If a draft feels like it
  needs a superlative, the chart is wrong, not the adjectives.
- **Stay out of fights.** No altcoin dunking threads, no quote-post ratios, no replying to bait. The
  two-sided brand is worth more than any single dunk. Disagreement is fine when it's arithmetic.
- **Disclaimers:** the tools carry the formal language; posts stay educational in framing ("run it
  yourself," "the model says, under this assumption"). Never personalized ("you should own X BTC").

## 4. Format ladder (ranked by leverage-for-effort)

1. **The anchor thread** (weekly) — a page decomposed into 5–8 posts, chart-first (§5 template).
2. **Single-shot insight + chart** — one camera export (the site's export pipeline produces clean
   2256×900 dark-theme images), one line of insight, tool link in a reply. The site's live-computed
   charts are inherently screenshot-worthy — this format is why that matters.
3. **Live-state posts** — the site computes fresh content every day: "Bitcoin sits at 0.43× its
   long-run trend today — a 57% discount. Every past stretch this deep resolved within 21 months.
   The record, not a promise." Post when the state *changes meaningfully* (multiple crosses a
   threshold, new backtest row triggers), not on a fixed schedule — scarcity keeps them signal.
4. **Value-add replies** — a relevant number or chart dropped into a bigger account's conversation,
   answering rather than promoting. The tool link goes in only if someone asks or it's directly
   responsive. This is the primary discovery mechanism until the account has its own gravity.
5. **Quote-posts of news** — when news touches a page's territory (a lender blows up → Borrowing;
   an STRC move → Fixed Income), quote with the arithmetic and the tool. Timeliness borrowed,
   rigor owned.
6. **Video clips** — derivative of the YouTube engine once it runs (Phase 2; REACH_GROWTH_PLAN owns
   the pipeline).

Mechanics that matter on today's X: post charts natively (never bare links — links go in the first
reply or after the hook post); alt-text every chart (accessibility + indexing); threads open with the
payoff, not the setup; pin the current anchor thread.

## 5. The thread factory

**Decomposition template** (any page → one thread):
1. **Hook** — the single most surprising verified number, stated flat, chart attached.
2. **Mechanism** — 2–3 posts: how the number is computed, in plain words, charts carrying the load.
3. **The honesty beat** — the negative case, unprompted (the backtest at the tops; the never-reverts
   case; the drawdown). This is the post that earns the bookmark and the follow.
4. **Both-sides close** — what the lens does NOT say; assumptions named.
5. **CTA** — "run it yourself," link, note that it's free/live/no-paywall.

**First-eight inventory** (each maps to a live page; hooks use current figures — refresh at drafting):
1. **Discount, or Premium?** (Mon Jul 27 — drafted, refreshing publish-day) — hook: 0.43× trend;
   follow-up beat day 3: the duration record (median ~14 mo, longest ~21).
2. **The Bitcoin Retirement** — hook: the single stack-size number that sustains a chosen income —
   and the scenario where it depletes (honesty beat built in). Top-traffic page; earliest candidate.
3. **Bull & Bear Cycles** — hook: four completed cycles, every one −75%+, every one followed by a
   new ATH — and the recovery multiples shrinking each time (both sides in one chart).
4. **Bitcoin vs. The Stock Market** — hook: the full-history comparison including the drawdowns.
5. **The Power Law / The Channel** — hook: 17 years inside one channel; what would falsify it
   (the caveats ARE the hook — nobody else leads with falsifiability).
6. **Wait, or Deploy Now?** — hook: what waiting for a deeper discount actually costs while the
   trend rises (anti-timing both ways).
7. **The Doubling Ladder** — hook: each doubling takes longer than the last — the declining-CAGR
   story told structurally.
8. **Borrowing Against Your Stack** — hook: what a −77% bear does to a loan's LTV (risk-tool
   framing; pairs with any lender news).

Rotation guardrail: threads recycle pages on a ~2-month cycle with fresh live numbers — the site's
figures move, so reruns are honest updates, not reposts.

## 6. The relationship layer (the compounding asset)

**Circles, by affinity to the work** (JM names specific accounts; categories here):
- **Power-law / quant circle** — Porkopolis (Mežinskis; already credited on The Channel — the
  warmest possible opening), Santostasi-adjacent power-law researchers, the chartist accounts that

- **Fixed-income / treasury circle** — Walton, Krueger (both already credited in the site's STRC

- **Tool-friendly educators** — accounts that reshare calculators and visuals to normie-curious

**Rules of engagement:** value before any ask, always — a useful number in their replies, a credit
in our threads, a genuine question. No cold DMs; DMs only after real public rapport. The asks this
layer eventually carries (reference letters ~Aug, Satos peer-nomination now, podcast intros later)
should arrive as natural extensions of visible mutual respect, not as transactions. Track lightly:
a short list in this doc's future revisions of who has engaged back, so warmth is real, not imagined.

**The capture loop:** any notable reshare, kind words from a credible account, or user testimonial
gets filed into `CREATOR_CREDIBILITY_KIT.md` §2 the same day ("application gold" — feeds
OpenSats impact, Satos, and every future partner conversation).

## 7. Publish-day runbook (first run: Monday Jul 27)

Pre-flight (before posting): X-card scrape done for the page (and ideally the four retitled pages);
thread numbers verified against the live page that morning (Claude delivers refreshed drafts);
charts exported fresh from the live tools; alt-text written.
Launch: post in the US-morning/EU-afternoon overlap window; charts native; link in reply #1; pin
the thread.
First hour: reply to every substantive response with numbers or the relevant page — the algorithm
and the relationship layer both reward presence.
Day 3: the follow-up beat (for the pilot: the duration record — "the question everyone asked:
how long does reversion take? Here's every episode in the record").
Day 7: harvest — questions people actually asked become FAQ candidates for the page and hooks for
future posts; file capture-loop items; 15-min review (§8).

## 8. Metrics & weekly review (15 minutes, honest funnel — not vanity)

Weekly: X referral sessions in GA (the number that matters — baseline ≈ zero, so every gain is
visible); bookmarks/saves per thread (the rigor-audience signal, worth more than likes); reshares
by credible accounts (capture-loop trigger); replies from the target circles (relationship pulse);
profile-visit → site-click ratio. Monthly: fold into the GA review — is X referral traffic trending
toward the reach-hinge goal? Adjust format mix toward whatever earns bookmarks and referral clicks,
not impressions. Explicitly ignored: follower count as a goal (it's an output), impressions on
reply-farming, ratio wins.

## 9. What would change this playbook

Signals to revisit at the ~monthly review: a format consistently out-earning the others (double
down); the relationship layer producing inbound (podcast invites, collab asks — reprioritize toward
it); X referral traffic stalling at zero after 6–8 consistent weeks (diagnose: hooks? charts?
timing? — before adding volume); Nostr showing independent signal (give it its own lane); JM's
enjoyment reading as effort rather than energy (cut cadence before cutting quality — sustainability
is the strategy).
