How matching works. A store bottle counts when the curated producer's name appears in the bottle's title, the grape or appellation fits the varietal, any named cuvée is present, and nothing else in the title points to a different cuvée. Other cuvées from the same producer are left out.

Accuracy. A 60-bottle spot check found about 9 in 10 matches clearly right. Most errors come from curated entries with no cuvée name (about a third of the library), where the matcher cannot tell which of a producer's wines the panel meant.

Availability. Each store's own catalogue says the bottle is available. Nothing here confirms shelf stock. Wine Chateau lists a very large distributor-backed range, so some of its bottles may ship with a delay. Out-of-state shipping varies by state and is not filtered yet.
