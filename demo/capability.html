The problem, in the industry’s own numbers

In its targeted exam of crypto communications, FINRA reviewed 500+ retail communications and found potential Rule 2210 violations in roughly 70% — unbalanced comparisons, misleading protection claims, omitted risks. Meanwhile the advisor channel has moved: 42% of advisors can now buy bitcoin in client accounts, but only 32% allocate — and 99% of those who do plan to maintain or increase (Bitwise/VettaFi survey, 2026). The gap between permitted and practicing is education an advisor can defend and a compliance officer can approve — and the people who buy advisor training say so themselves:

“I’m not sure I understand what I would put into a formal training program around these assets. That’s one of the big problems.”

— Adam Handler, VP of Product Management, Axtella (WealthManagement.com)

What I build

The thing that goes in that box. At lastcoinstanding.com I have designed, built, and maintain 34 live-computed interactive tools — allocation sizing, retirement sustainability, drawdown stress tests, asset comparisons, market-cycle context — every one of them:

  • Both sides, always — risks stated unprompted; the honest case, not the embellished one

  • Source-substantiated — a maintained audit log for every figure (the discipline FINRA Rule 2210(b) requires firms to keep)
  • Educational, never promotional — no recommendations, no product steering

One person, end-to-end: concept, analytical design, build, editorial, maintenance. Before this: ten years as a Director of Product at Mastercard.

Built for your compliance desk, not around it

Non-promotional educational content is the cheapest class of communication a firm can adopt: it can qualify for exemption from prior principal approval (FINRA 2210(b)(1)(D)(iii)), and generic educational content is exempt from FINRA filing (Reg. Notice 16-41). Delivery is self-contained static files your firm hosts — interactive for the user, inert for your network: no system access, no client data, no external dependencies. Vendor onboarding without the security-review tail.
