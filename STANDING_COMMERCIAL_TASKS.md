## Conventions

- **Ledger entries are append-only and dated** `YYYY-MM-DD`, newest at the bottom of the section.
- **Record what was sent, to whom, and the next action with its date** — an entry without a next
  action is a dead end, not a record.
- **Verified vs. unverified** is stated explicitly for contact details and titles. An address
  assumed from a pattern is not the same as one confirmed, and the difference decides whether a
  fallback is a fallback or a guess.
- Personal contact details, message bodies and anything a recipient would not expect to see written
  down stay **out of this file** — it is tracked in a public repository. Record the fact and the
  shape, not the content.
