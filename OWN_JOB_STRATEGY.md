## 2. The two structural rules (get these right and everything else is tactics)

**Rule 1 — Sell the capability, never the site.** The pitch is: *"I built this entire platform
solo — ideation, analytical design, live-computed tools, editorial discipline, maintenance
pipeline. Imagine that capability aimed at your brand, your compliance envelope, your
distribution."* Never: "you can have my site." The site stays independent under every outcome —
it's what makes the proof-of-work credible, and it's the fallback if any engagement ends. Licensing
(door 3) is the only model that touches the site's assets, and it licenses *copies under their
brand*, never editorial control of lastcoinstanding.com.

**Rule 2 — The register IS the differentiator; don't sand it off in the pitch.** What a compliance
department at a regulated asset manager fears most about bitcoin content is over-claiming. JM's
entire discipline — both sides always, risks unprompted, every number reproducible, no
embellishment — is precisely what survives compliance review. The pitch should lead with this:
*"my content is built to the standard your legal team wishes vendors wrote to."* Fidelity's own
"Getting Off Zero" language and the site's register are already convergent; that's the wedge.

## 5. Practical/compliance notes (know before first contact)

- **Outside-business-activity disclosure:** any employment at a broker-dealer-affiliated or RIA
  firm will require disclosing the site; some will want review rights or constraints on it. Decide
  the red line in advance: **the site's independence is non-negotiable; its cadence is not.**
  (Slowing the site during an engagement is acceptable; handing over editorial control is not.)
- **No registered-rep requirement** for content/education roles generally — but licensing (Series
  65/7) could be asked for research-adjacent titles. Worth knowing JM's appetite before NYDIG-type
  conversations.
- **The disclaimer discipline already on the site** (educational, never personalized) is the
  correct posture in every interview and sample artifact.
